# Step 5 verification

October 4, 2026. Local production build only, served by the single Node process at localhost:5174. No deployment. No scores, match length, typed radio, quick phrases or additional layouts.

## Changes

Human hiders leave a footprint approximately every 0.45 metres of accepted street movement throughout hiding and seeking. Hiding prints begin their 60-second linear fade when seeking begins; seeking prints fade from creation. Each room stores at most 256 prints, four disturbed marks, three radio transmissions and one three-option clue offer. Expired prints are removed. Sustained walking can evict the oldest prints before their full lifetime, retaining roughly 115 metres of recent travel.

Burial makes one disturbed patch. After burial the contextual action offers Disturb at other reachable spots, up to three decoys in hiding or seeking. The server enforces role, phase, range, round identity, uniqueness and capacity. Hiders never get Search. Solo rooms create two collision-valid routes, one to the burial and one to a decoy, plus three decoy marks. A cryptographic shuffle prevents the array's first route identifying the real trail.

Footprints and soil are pooled instanced ground geometry. Fragments outside every seeker's exact shared cone are discarded, so moonlight never reveals them. The final renderer also respects existing spotlight shadows. Dark earth soles contrast against illuminated paving. Accepted server poses give all devices the same geometric reveal gate; real light motion remains interpolated. World ground heights for road, paving, lawns, driveways and porch landings are shared between rendering and server freeze checks.

The server freezes a human hider in a seeker's cone for two seconds, then grants five seconds of immunity after the freeze. It rejects translation while frozen and permits turning. Both screens show Frozen on the hider label; their own instruction changes while frozen. During seeking the hider has no flashlight and can keep laying misleading footprints when unfrozen.

At 30, 60 and 90 seconds into seeking, the hider receives three true clue choices. They have five seconds to choose, otherwise the server sends one. Solo or disconnected hiders send automatically at the scheduled time. Only the hider receives the offer. Each possible combination of offered clues retains at least two candidate spots, including the capsule. Transmissions appear for 15 seconds with a short procedural crackle, respecting mute and hidden-page audio. The bounded clue history survives refresh. This is scheduled clue delivery, not radio chat.

Phase and countdown remain compact at the top centre. Instructions are at the bottom between stick and action. Three persistent clue buttons fit the same centre space with at least 44-pixel targets. The capsule label is reserved in player-label placement, avoiding reveal overlap. Canvas, stick, action and clue controls are created once; snapshots update their state.

## Limits and scope

Physical Android/iPhone FPS, sustained heat/battery, real thumb comfort, mobile browser chrome, lock/unlock and cellular/WAN behaviour remain unverified. Touch and viewport checks are desktop browser emulation. Only two beams cast dynamic shadows; other beams retain the previously documented light leakage through solids. The reveal gate uses accepted poses while light movement is smoothed locally, so beam edges can differ transiently by roughly one broadcast/interpolation window. Freeze uses the shared geometric cone rather than server wall visibility filtering.

Footprints and dirt are simple authored geometry, not final textured art. Sixty seconds and the clue wording need group playtests. Capacity may evict old prints early. The furniture room still validates its outer bounds but has no decorative furniture colliders. Full trace arrays currently travel in room snapshots; mobile bandwidth at sustained maximum trails has not been measured. The Vite bundle-size warning remains. These are not claims of a shipped phone performance target.

No step 6 code, deployment or purchases. Existing asset source filenames and licences are unchanged. PLAN.md and project instructions now describe the approved step 5 scope and exact lifetime/bounds.
