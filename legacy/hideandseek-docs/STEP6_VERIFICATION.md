## Ordering and capsule secrecy

Public snapshots now independently Fisher-Yates shuffle both disturbed marks and footprints using Node crypto.randomInt. This happens for broadcasts and action results, including after a human adds a decoy during seeking. It does not mutate the stored collection. Identifiers, positions and mark timestamps remain stable; list indices have no relationship to creation order. Previously the source sorted marks by random UUID; public projection now explicitly removes reliance on storage order instead of assuming that source arrangement is sufficient.

Ten complete server-suite runs sampled 600 actual solo rounds. After reveal, tests mapped each capsule back to the earlier seeker mark list: the real mark appeared at indices 0/1/2/3 respectively **143/151/161/145** times. All four positions occurred in every 60-round run. The first public footprint changed between consecutive projections in 593 of those 600 rounds. Solo generation already randomized which of the two routes was generated first; its hiding prints share the seeking-start fade clock. Fresh projection also removes route-concatenation order. The routes remain physically readable and stable on screen because footprint side depends on identifier, not array index.

A human-round test buries first, adds a decoy after seeking starts, and checks twenty action-result projections: the burial occurs in both indices and the complete ID/position/timestamp set stays identical. Captured pre-reveal seeker broadcasts and replies contain no capsuleSpotId, clueOffer, decoysRemaining, real/false/source flag, mark spot ID or secret seed. Footprint fields are exactly id/x/z/facing/fadeAt/expiresAt; marks are exactly id/x/z/createdAt. Capsule disclosure begins at reveal.

Random order prevents an index from tagging the real mark; it cannot erase observations. A human who creates no decoys leaves one informative disturbed patch until other marks are created. Walking paths, clues and successive observations can legitimately help deduce a spot. A modified client can inspect the unlabelled geometry before lighting it, as allowed by the design; search completion remains server-validated.

## Matches and scores

One person plays three rounds; two play four alternating-hider rounds; three through six each have one hider turn. The server owns the scoring roster, match identity, round count, result and winners. On a find every participating seeker receives remaining whole seeking seconds, and the hider receives elapsed seconds rounded down. These partition the production 120-second seeking time. Timeout awards the hider 120 and seekers zero. Solo has no human hider award. Reveal changes phase once, so repeated completion requests cannot award twice.

The persistent score panel shows result, round/total, latest award and running totals. Eight seconds after a normal reveal, the server starts the next round without a request; the host can advance after the five-second capsule reveal. The last reveal finishes the match instead. Final winners have explicit Winner text, a closing summer line, Play again and Back to title. Any seated player can replay; duplicate replay requests cannot start several matches. Global round epochs continue increasing across matches, preventing old actions from becoming current after scores reset.

Mid-round arrivals explore preparation until the next boundary. Join, explicit leave or seat expiry changes membership and begins a fresh announced scoring cycle on that boundary. Temporary disconnection retains the reserved seat and scores; disconnected future hiders are skipped without pausing other players. A departed hider's current round still runs to reveal. Roster changes are announced on the between-round panel and at the first hiding phase of the fresh cycle.

Only an authoritative solo final total updates the browser-local best. Tests seed a prior best of 17 and verify a zero-point match does not erase it, including final-screen refresh. This best is personal device storage, not a trusted multiplayer score. Storage unavailable or cleared means it cannot persist.
